## Seongwon Janggi 0.3.0

APK for Android (arm64, Android 7.1 or later). To install it from this page, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.

### What's new
- **Stronger AI**: the latest Fairy-Stockfish engine with the janggi neural network (NNUE). Level 9 plays at the engine's full strength, and levels 1–9 have been re-spaced so beginners can climb one level at a time.
- **Feel of the pieces**: a wooden clack and vibration the moment a piece lands, move speed that depends on distance, and captured pieces knocked aside.
- **Controls**: drag-and-drop moves, illegal targets make the piece shake, and the AI lifts its piece with a gold ring before moving.
- **Review and sharing**: step back and forth through the game and see the engine's move for any position; share the move record as text.
- **Screen**: the board fills the screen width, captured pieces appear in the player panels, and a bar shows the AI's thinking time.
- **Record**: two-player games no longer count toward the win/loss record, which can now be reset in Settings.
- Vibration on/off, and rule sets (casual, bikjang draw, tournament).
